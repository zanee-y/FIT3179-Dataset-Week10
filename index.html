Water Production Distribution

Where Is Water Production Highest?

Selangor records the highest water production capacity, followed closely by Johor and Perak. These states have the infrastructure to support their larger populations and industrial activities.

Smaller states like Perlis and W.P. Labuan maintain more modest production levels, reflecting their population sizes and development patterns.

These results highlight Malaysia's strategic water management approach, where production capacity aligns with regional needs and development priorities.

Key Insights:
• Selangor: Highest production, supports largest population
• Johor: Second highest, industrial and residential demand
• Perak: Strong production for agricultural and urban needs
• W.P. Labuan: Lowest production, smallest population
