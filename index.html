Malaysia Water Consumption Analysis (2022)

Interactive visualizations exploring water usage patterns across Malaysian states. Data shows domestic and non-domestic water consumption in million liters.

Water Consumption by State and Sector

This choropleth map shows water consumption across Malaysian states. Use the dropdown to switch between domestic and non-domestic water usage. Darker blue indicates higher consumption levels. Selangor has the highest consumption at 2,230 million liters for domestic use.

Total Water Consumption by State

This bar chart displays the total water consumption (domestic + non-domestic) for each state. Hover over bars to see exact values. States are sorted by total consumption, showing Selangor as the highest consumer with 3,580 million liters combined.
