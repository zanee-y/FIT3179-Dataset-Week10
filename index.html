Malaysia Water Consumption Analysis (2022)

Interactive visualization exploring water usage patterns across Malaysian states. Data shows domestic and non-domestic water consumption in million liters.

Water Consumption by State and Sector

This choropleth map shows water consumption across Malaysian states. Use the dropdown to switch between domestic and non-domestic water usage. Darker blue indicates higher consumption levels. Selangor has the highest consumption at 2,230 million liters for domestic use.

Interactions:

  • Use the dropdown filter to switch between Domestic and Non-Domestic sectors
  • Hover over states to see detailed consumption information
  • Text annotation highlights the highest consumption area
